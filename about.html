Standing on new ground

New Ground Australia is a youth-oriented movement and media brand. We advocate for Christian values in Australian public life, and we encourage the next generation to engage.

Why we exist

We began by speaking, in broad terms, about Australia’s future and the kind of country younger people want to inherit. That was true as far as it went, and it was not yet clear enough. We are now explicit about the ground we stand on.

We believe Christian faith is a gift to public life, not a private hobby to be hidden away. It has something to say about family, the worth of every person, truth-telling, service, and the freedom to live what you believe. We want to say those things with hope, and to invite the next generation to take them seriously.

What we do

We make content and commentary. We talk with churches, ministries and individuals. We encourage younger Australians to take part in civic life: to think, to speak, to serve locally, and to stay engaged when public debate is frustrating.

New Ground Australia is not currently a legal entity. We are not a church, not a political party, and not a registered charity or not-for-profit.

How we talk with people

People of all backgrounds are welcome to read, question and talk with us. We will disagree with ideas when we think they matter. We will do it without contempt for the people who hold them. If you are curious, unconvinced, or already convinced, there is room for a respectful conversation.
