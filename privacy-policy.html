Privacy Policy

This policy explains what we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated 25 October 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by New Ground Australia. New Ground Australia is not currently a legal entity. We are not a church, not a political party, and not a registered charity or not-for-profit. For privacy questions, email newgroundaus@gmail.com.

Enquiry form

If you use the Get Involved form, we collect:

  • whether you are a church or ministry, an individual, an organisation or business, media, or other
  • the kinds of help you are offering
  • your name and email address
  • your phone number, church or organisation, suburb or city, and state, if you choose to give them
  • your message
  • your confirmation that we may contact you about the enquiry

We use this information to read your enquiry and reply. The form is sent by email to newgroundaus@gmail.com through EmailJS. We do not store form submissions in a database on this website.

Prayer requests

If you use the prayer request form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and what you would like us to pray for, plus your confirmation that we may contact you about the request.

We use this so our team can pray and get in touch. The form is emailed to newgroundaus@gmail.com through EmailJS. We do not store prayer requests in a database on this website.

We keep enquiry and prayer emails in the newgroundaus@gmail.com inbox so we can reply and follow up. The people who answer that inbox can read them. To ask for access or deletion, email newgroundaus@gmail.com from the address you used, and say what you want deleted. We will delete the email and related replies we still hold, unless we need to keep something to meet a legal duty.

Donations

Card donations are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects the payment details needed to take the gift. We do not receive or store your full card number on this website. Donation records may include your name, email, amount and a payment reference, as provided by Stripe.

New Ground Australia is not currently a legal entity, so a gift is not paid to a registered organisation. Card payments are processed by Stripe.

Website analytics and technical data

This site uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-ZFMCHPX72S). Google may collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser details, and a client identifier. You can read Google’s policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not keep our own database of visitors. The service that hosts this website may store ordinary technical logs, such as which page was requested and when.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the home page embeds a public Instagram reel. Those services may receive your IP address and set their own cookies when the content loads.

Who else sees information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as needed to run the site and respond to you:

  • EmailJS, to email us your enquiry or prayer request
  • Stripe, to process a card donation
  • Google, for Analytics and fonts
  • Instagram, when the embedded reel loads
  • our email provider, which holds the newgroundaus@gmail.com inbox

EmailJS, Stripe, Google and Instagram may process information outside Australia. We do not control which countries they use.

If you enable the optional spam check later, Cloudflare Turnstile may process a token to help us reject automated submissions. It is off unless we switch it on.

Security

Email is not a perfectly private channel. Please do not send sensitive identity documents or payment card numbers in the enquiry form. Card payments stay on Stripe’s page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
