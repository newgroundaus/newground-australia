Donations & Refunds Policy

Gifts to New Ground Australia are voluntary. This page explains what they are, and what to do if a payment was a mistake or a duplicate.

Last updated 25 October 2026.

Voluntary gifts

A donation supports our work of advocating for Christian values in Australia, including content, commentary, community engagement and running costs. It is not a purchase of goods, and it does not create a contract for services.

Donations are not tax-deductible. New Ground Australia is not currently a legal entity, so a gift is not a donation to a charity.

How you can give

You can give by card through the Stripe payment button on the Donate page. Card details are handled by Stripe, not stored on this website.

New Ground Australia is not currently a legal entity, so a gift is not paid to a registered organisation. Card payments are processed by Stripe. We will update this page if that changes.

Mistaken or duplicate donations

If a card payment was a mistake, or the same amount was taken twice, email newgroundaus@gmail.com. Include the date, the amount, and any receipt reference you have. Please do not send a full card number.

We will look at the request and reply by email. We cannot promise a refund in every case.

Contact

Refund questions go to the same address: newgroundaus@gmail.com.
