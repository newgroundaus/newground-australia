Support the work

Donations support advocating for Christian values in Australia: content creation, commentary, community engagement, and the ordinary costs of running New Ground Australia.

What a gift supports

Your gift helps us keep making content, talking with churches and communities, and covering the day-to-day costs of the work. We are not publishing a spending breakdown, because we will not invent one.

A donation is voluntary. It does not buy goods or services. Donations are not tax-deductible. New Ground Australia is not currently a legal entity, so a gift is not a donation to a charity.

Where the money goes

New Ground Australia is not currently a legal entity, so a gift is not paid to a registered organisation. Card payments are processed by Stripe. We will update this page if that changes.

Nothing on this page is a request for a tax-deductible gift. If you gave by mistake, or a payment was taken twice, please read the donations and refunds policy and contact us.
