Why this matters

Faith has been part of Australian public life for a long time. The Commonwealth Constitution opens by “humbly relying on the blessing of Almighty God”, and section 116 limits the Commonwealth’s power to establish a religion or to prohibit its free exercise. Many schools, hospitals and welfare services in this country began through churches. That history is not a claim that everyone must believe the same things. It is a reminder that Christian conviction has helped shape how Australians talk about dignity, duty, family and the neighbour.

Those convictions still matter for the future. Children need households where they are known and loved. Communities hold together when people tell the truth and look after one another. A free country needs room for people to live and speak their faith openly, including in schools, workplaces and public debate. We want the next generation to inherit that freedom and to use it well: in their families, their churches, and their civic life.

New Ground Australia exists to say this plainly, and to invite younger Australians to engage rather than sit the conversation out.

What we stand for

Six commitments shape our commentary and our work with churches and communities.

Faith and freedom

Room to live and speak one’s faith in public, with the same courtesy offered to others.

Family

Marriage, parenthood and strong households as a foundation for children and community.

Dignity of life

Every person made in God’s image, worthy of protection at every stage.

Truth and courage

Honesty and integrity, spoken with grace rather than contempt.

Community and service

Loving your neighbour through the local church, practical care, and civic life.

Responsible citizenship

Work, honesty and personal responsibility, grounded in Christian ethics.

Churches and communities

We want to connect with churches, ministries and Christians across Australia. If you can help by inviting us to speak, hosting a conversation, volunteering, offering a skill, sharing a venue, or supporting the work in prayer, we would like to hear from you.

Individuals are just as welcome as churches. You do not need a title or a platform. A willing pair of hands, a thoughtful question, or a prayer is a real contribution.

If you need prayer, send a request and our team will be praying for you.
